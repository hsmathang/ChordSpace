1. Definicion Formal de $d_{\text{EB}}$

Sea $K = \max(|\text{supp}(A)|, |\text{supp}(B)|)$ donde $\text{supp}$ usa tolerancia $\epsilon = 10^{-6}$ st para comparar floats.

$$d_{\text{EB}}(A,B) = \min_{A' \in E_K(A),\, B' \in E_K(B)} \min_{\sigma \in S_K} \frac{1}{K} \sum_{i=1}^K \text{step}(A'_i, B'_{\sigma(i)})$$

$\text{step}(x,y) = \min(|x \bmod 12 - y \bmod 12|, 12 - |\cdots|)$ opera sobre valores MIDI continuos ($\mathbb{R}$).
